Designing an all-in-one learning platform

A complete user experience for a platform that combines learning content, an online shop and a course centre, built around smooth navigation.

Discipline
UX/UI
Duration
6 months
Client
Education start-up
+75%User engagement lift
−40%Bounce rate reduction
90%Customer satisfaction

Overview

The goal was a single platform for e-learning, e-commerce and course management. The core challenge was keeping it simple for very different people, from students to coaches to shop owners.

We focused on an easy interface, with a modern visual style that never competes with the content.

How it came together

Three phases, each one testing the last.

  1. Research & analysis

    User interviews and competitor analysis to understand needs and gaps in the market.

  2. First designs

    Wireframes and prototypes to test the key user flows.

  3. Build & test

    Final interfaces, testing with users, and improvements based on their feedback.

Challenges & solutions

Pick a challenge to see the design decision that solved it.

Many kinds of user

Serving students, coaches and sellers in one interface without making it complicated.

Solution

A dedicated flow for each group, with dynamic navigation that shows each person only what they need.

Performance

Course pages are full of images and video, and a slow page loses a student before the lesson starts.

Solution

Media loads as people scroll and images are sized for their slot, so the first screen appears quickly and reading isn’t interrupted.

Integration

The online shop and the course centre were separate systems, but students shouldn’t feel the seam.

Solution

One journey from buying a course to taking it, so both feel like the same product, with unified APIs keeping the two sides in sync.
