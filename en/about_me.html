A designer who came from engineering

A little about me, and why I care about turning ideas into visual experiences that do their job.

I love the space where visual beauty meets practical function.

I'm Ahmed Omer, a designer from Sudan with an honours degree in engineering, now living and working in Abu Dhabi. I started designing in 2013 and haven't stopped exploring since.

I specialise in brand identities and in designing interfaces and user experiences that make digital products smooth and enjoyable to use.

Engineering taught me that every element has to earn its place. So I don't add a colour, a typeface or an animation unless it serves the user or the message.
